# Settlements reel: script and subtitles

Silent cut: `settlements-reel-silent-9x16.mp4`, 27.5 seconds, timings in the table below.
Voice cuts: `settlements-reel-voice-9x16.mp4` and `settlements-reel-voice-4x5.mp4`, 41.5 seconds, same lines timed to Yonatan's reading. He read lines 1 to 7; the end card runs silent.
The year on screen runs slower through the busy years, so each line sits on the years it talks about.
Layout: subtitles at the top (they replace the title after the first second), a big map across the frame, and the year, period and count over the sea. No bar chart.
