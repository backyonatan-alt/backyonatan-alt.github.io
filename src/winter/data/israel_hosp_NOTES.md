# Israel weekly new RSV / flu inpatients: notes

Source: Israel MoH / ICDC weekly respiratory surveillance PDFs on gov.il (read via WebFetch). All rows come from MoH PDFs; no news-site figures were used. Nothing estimated or interpolated.

## Coverage (not comparable across seasons)
- **2023/24:** "general hospitals in Israel", with 1 to 5 hospitals not reporting each week. Total base never stated. Stored as "all general hospitals minus N not reporting".
- **Summer 2024 (Aug, out of range):** counts based on Clalit hospitals only.
- **2024/25:** "based on 13 hospitals nationwide" (about 60% of general hospital beds). Week 46 says 12 hospitals; week 2 only 12 of 13 (technical fault); week 50 one hospital missing.
- So 2024/25 counts cover a smaller base than 2023/24. Don't compare raw levels across seasons.

## Gaps
- 2023/24 missing weeks: 43, 46, 47, 48, 12, 16, 18, 19, 20. Week 12: the 23/03/2024 report repeats week-11 hospital data. Week 40 flu left blank (report gives 24 and 19).
- 2024/25 missing weeks: 42, 44, 47, 5, 10, 15, 16, 17 (week 15-16 is Passover 2025).
- Missing = gov.il returned 4xx for every URL variant tried. Filenames vary: 2023/24 English files end in "e.pdf" (e.g. flu-13012024e.pdf), one folder is misspelled (flu-09120223).
- Flu totals: some weeks only state influenza A in the hospital section (flagged in note column).
- 2023/24 week 5 RSV (334) is identical to week 4, as printed.
- 2023/24 week 15 RSV sentence says "As of week 14", so may not be week 15 alone.
- 2022/23 not collected. A JDN article (21/12/2022) quotes 333 RSV and 289 flu inpatients for the latest week, and 302 / 315 the week before.

## Age shares
- No report checked gives an age breakdown or share under 2 for RSV inpatients.
- The only under-2 figure is ED visits for bronchiolitis, e.g. "increase in the share of ED visits by 0-2 year olds for bronchiolitis" (week 40, 2024; translated).
