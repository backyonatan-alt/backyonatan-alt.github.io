# cohort_figures.csv notes

One row per number, as stated by the source. No derived rates. Where a ratio was not printed (Monto), the raw counts are stored instead. Fetch date: 2026-11-08. PubMed/PMC pages were often blocked for the fetcher, so some abstracts were read from mirrors (scilit, zhenzhi, qxmd, intramed, BVS). Check those against the journal before publishing.

## Helsinki municipal daycare (Ponka et al. 1991, Infection 19:230-6)
Prospective, 29 Helsinki municipal daycare centres, 1985-86, 1,905 follow-up years. Illness periods per follow-up year: 7.9 under 3, 3.8 older, 4.9 overall. Illness days 39, 17, 23. Diagnosis mix of periods: URTI 46.0%, diarrhea 17.2%, otitis 12.9%, eye 4.0%, tonsillitis 3.2%, bronchitis 3.0%. Note: the journal is *Infection*, not Eur J Pediatr (DOI 10.1007/bf01644951 resolves to Infection). Part II (Infection 19:331-5) is the cost paper.
Supports: an episodes-per-year and sick-days anchor for under-3s in centre care, and a cause mix that includes stomach bugs. Can't support: home vs daycare (no home group), split of 1-2 vs 0-1 within "under 3", seasonality. Old (1985-86), pre-pneumococcal and rotavirus vaccines, Finnish ventilation and group sizes. Counts absence periods, not every cold.

## WHISTLER (de Hoog et al. 2014, BMC Med 12:107)
Utrecht birth cohort, born 2001-2012, 1,728 with complete data (1,378 daycare in first year, 350 not), followed to 6. Outcome is GP-diagnosed URTI + AOM (new episode needs 28 disease-free days). Table 1 rates per 100 child-years, home vs daycare: 0-1 y 59.1 vs 100.4; 1-2 y 71.2 vs 77.6; 2-3 y 52.5 vs 44.2; 3-4 y 38.5 vs 31.5; 4-5 y 41.7 vs 25.0; 5-6 y 41.6 vs 20.4. Six-year totals 56.2 vs 59.2 (aIRR 1.08, n.s.).
Supports: the "pay now or pay later" story. Excess in year 1, near zero at 1-2, reversal from 4. Can't support: total colds (only doctor visits, roughly one in 5-10 colds), daycare start after age 1 (exposure is first-year daycare only), seasonality.

## Quebec (Cote et al. 2010, Arch Pediatr Adolesc Med 164:1132-7)
Quebec Longitudinal Study of Child Development, born 1998, 1,238 analysed, mother-reported infections over 3-month recall, yearly to age 8. Large centres (groups of 8-12) before 2.5 y vs home: IRR 1.61 febrile respiratory and 1.62 otitis in preschool; 0.79 and 0.57 at ages 5-8. No GI effect. The IRRs with CIs come from a Spanish summary of the abstract (intramed); the 1.61/1.62/0.79/0.57 ratios also appear in a CBC report. The full abstract on PubMed could not be read. One extra row (small group OM IRR 1.47 at 3.5-4.5 y) conflicts with news write-ups that say small groups showed no difference; verify before using.
Supports: relative risk and the later payback, large centre vs small group. Can't support: absolute rates (not obtained), 1-2 y vs other ages separately.

## Pittsburgh (Wald et al. 1988 J Pediatr 112:540-6; 1991 J Pediatr 3-year follow-up; 1991 Pediatrics 87:129-33)
Birth cohort in home care, group care (2-6 children) and day care (7+), phone survey every 2 weeks, 3 years. The 1988 abstract was not reachable and the 1991 J Pediatr abstract has no rates (only: infections peak in year 2, daycare-home gap narrows in year 3). The 1991 Pediatrics paper gives URI duration: mean 6.6 days (1-2 y, home) to 8.9 days (<1 y, day care); share lasting over 15 days 6.5% to 13.1%; 29.2% of 2,741 infections complicated by otitis.
Supports: episode length and "daycare colds last a bit longer". Can't support: episodes per year (not obtained). Old (1980s), small.

## Denmark national registers (Soegaard et al. 2023, Int J Epidemiol 52:466-75)
1,007,448 children born 1997-2014, outcome = antimicrobial prescriptions. Shortly after enrolment at 6 months: 1.9 (boys) / 1.5 (girls) treated infections per year in creche. At 12 months, 0.6-0.8 more per year than home care (+66-68%); at 3 y, +0.3 (+48%). Enrolment at 6-12 mo vs 3 y adds 0.5-0.7 cumulative infections by 6. No reversal at 6-13.
Supports: the transient spike after start, and that the long-run extra is small. Can't support: all illness (only antibiotic-treated), payback below home levels (not seen here).

## STEPS, Finland (Schuez-Havupalo et al. 2017 BMJ Open; Toivonen et al. 2016 Pediatrics; Toivonen et al. 2016 PIDJ)
Birth cohort, Southwest Finland, born 2008-2010, daily symptom diary to age 2. Mean daycare start age 15 months, so the closest match to a 1-2 year old starting gan. Centre care: RTI sick days per month 3.79 before start, 10.57 at 2 months after start, back to home-care level by month 5. Family daycare (max 5 kids): 3.53 to 8.34. Whole cohort: 5.9 ARIs per child per year (0-2 y), rhinovirus in 59% of tested ARIs and 3.5 rhinovirus infections per child-year. Top 10% had 98+ illness days per year.
Supports: the shape and size of the first-months spike, sick days per month, rhinovirus share. Can't support: episodes per year by care type (not reported), seasonality (38% started in fall-winter, but outcomes not split by season).

## LoewenKIDS, Germany (Langer et al. 2022, Microorganisms 10:111)
288 children with near-complete daily diaries, 2014-2020, 0-2 y. 13.7 ARIs in two years (6.0 in year 1, 7.7 in year 2). Median episode 9.7 days (IQR 7-14); episode ends after 3 symptom-free days. Childcare start at 13-26 months: 14.1 episodes vs 11.4 for no childcare (aRR 1.26). Symptom days 49.5 per child at 13-18 months, 43.4 at 19-24 months (per 6 months). Winter higher than summer, values only in a figure.
Supports: the best single source for the simulator. Episodes per year at age 1-2, episode length, symptom days. Can't support: seasonal share (figure only). Diaries count mild runny noses, so rates are much higher than doctor-based studies.

## PREVAIL, Cincinnati (Teoh et al. 2023, Clin Infect Dis 77:901-9)
245 infants, weekly swabs and texts, 2017-2020, 0-2 y. Viral ARI (cough or fever) 3.30 per child-year; rhinovirus/enterovirus 1.89, adenovirus 0.35, parainfluenza 3 0.24, RSV A+B 0.38, hMPV 0.16, flu A 0.11. Median symptom duration 5 days (RSV 7). Only 37% of viral infections had symptoms.
Supports: cause mix by virus with rates, short median duration under a stricter ARI definition. Can't support: daycare effect (not reported), GI. Overlaps COVID-19 lockdowns, which cut transmission.

## Duration review (Thompson et al. 2013, BMJ 347:f7027)
Systematic review, 48 studies, children seen in primary or emergency care. Days until 90% recovered: common cold 15, non-specific RTI 16, cough 25, earache 7-8, sore throat 2-7, bronchiolitis 21, croup 2.
Supports: the long tail of an episode (for the simulator's duration distribution). Can't support: median length (50% figures not obtained), age 1-2 specifically.

## Tecumseh (Monto & Sullivan 1993, Epidemiol Infect 110:145-60)
Michigan community, 1976-81. Ages 0-4: 2,657 illnesses in 539 person-years (ratio not printed; rates peak at 1-2 y per text). Agent mix all ages: rhinovirus 34%, coronavirus 14%, flu 9%, bacterial 8%, RSV 4%, parainfluenza 4%, adenovirus 2%, unknown 23%. Seasonal timing described in words only.
Supports: a classic community reference and cause mix. Can't support: daycare comparison, seasonal share as a number. Old, all ages for the mix.

## Smaller sources
- Vesa et al. 2001 (Finland, 329 children 2-24 mo): of 761 virus-positive URI episodes, rhinovirus 63.1%, RSV 14.7%, flu A 6.7%, PIV3 6.7%, adenovirus 6.2%; AOM in 43.4% of URIs.
- Chonmaitree et al. 2015 (Texas, 362 infants, first year): AOM complicated 27% of URTIs.
- Vissing et al. 2018 (COPSAC2000, 334 children): median 14 infections 0-3 y (range 2-43); each extra child in the daycare group raised URI rate 11%.
- Hullegie et al. 2016 (WHISTLER, GP-diagnosed gastroenteritis): 12.2 vs 13.3 per 100 child-years over 6 y; IRR 1.13 at age 1, lower in daycare kids at 3-6; excess greatest in first 12 months after entry.
- Sarna et al. 2016 (ORChID Brisbane, 154 infants): 0.56 ARIs per child-month, median runny nose 6 days, symptoms on 19.7% of days. Subtropical.
- Rindel et al. 1992 (24 Copenhagen nurseries): sick leave 7.5% of opening days, 14.6-24.4 sick days per child per year. Ages not stated.
- Fleming et al. 1987 (Atlanta phone survey): daycare OR 1.6 for URTI, 3.8 for ear infection in full-time attendees. Cross-sectional.

## Gaps
- No source found giving the share of episodes in winter months as a number. LoewenKIDS, ORChID and PREVAIL show it only in figures. Use the Israeli weekly surveillance files for season shape instead.
- Côté absolute rates and Wald 1988 numbers were not reachable.
