# Data Stories site

Yonatan's personal site of interactive data articles, served by GitHub Pages from the `main` branch.
Live at https://backyonatan-alt.github.io/

## Pages

| Address | What | Edit this | Then run |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | Home page, one row per article | `index.html` directly | nothing |
| `/cousins/` | A Nation of Cousins (animated Jewish population map) | `src/cousins/` (`page.html`, `app.js`, `data.js`) | `node src/cousins/build.js` |
| `/moneyball/`, `/moreyball/`, `/expected-goals/` | Three sports analytics articles | `src/sports/sports-analytics-by-the-numbers.html` (one combined source) | `python3 src/sports/split.py` |

Never hand-edit `cousins/index.html` or the three sports `index.html` files. They are build output and get overwritten.

## Rules for this site

- Every article has a "← All stories" link back to `/`. Articles do not link to each other.
- Every article opens with an abstract and a hook: a question or surprising fact, two or three short paragraphs of background for a reader who knows nothing about the subject, and what the page covers. The sports pages use the `.brief` block for this. New pages need one too.
- Home page: one full-width row per article, all equal, no sections. Stacks image over text on phones.
- Every page carries Google Analytics (`G-ZHETPL7EV5`). The build scripts add it. New pages need it too.
- Every page needs Open Graph tags and a 1200x630 preview image (`preview.png` or `cousins-preview.png`) for link previews.
- Light mode only. No dark-mode rules (`prefers-color-scheme`) in any article, so every reader sees the same colors. The dark map stage and dark home page are fixed designs, not a dark mode.
- Phones matter. Check every change at about 390px wide for sideways overflow before pushing.
- Copy style: short plain sentences, no em dashes.

## Cousins map notes

- `data.js` holds the dataset: 36 regions, 9 communities, populations in thousands at anchor years, plus chapters, events and migration arcs. `node src/cousins/check.js` prints world totals by year to compare against published figures.
- `map.json` is generated by `gen_map.js` from the `world-atlas` npm package. It rarely needs regenerating.
- Regional figures before 1800 are a reconstruction and have not been reviewed by a specialist. World totals from 1880 match DellaPergola / AJYB.

## Adding a new article

1. Make a folder at the root (`/name/`) with `index.html` and a `preview.png`.
2. Add the GA tag, OG tags, and the "← All stories" link.
3. Add a row to `index.html` and a line to `README.md`.

## Launch material

`launch/posts.md` has draft posts (LinkedIn, Reddit, Hebrew). `launch/cousins-map.mp4` is a 33-second playback video to attach to posts.
