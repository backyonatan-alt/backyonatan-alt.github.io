## Rules for this site

- Every article has a "← All stories" link back to `/`. Articles do not link to each other.
- Every article opens with an abstract and a hook: a question or surprising fact, two or three short paragraphs of background for a reader who knows nothing about the subject, and what the page covers. The sports pages use the `.brief` block for this. New pages need one too.
- Home page: one full-width row per article, all equal, no sections. Stacks image over text on phones.
- Every page carries Google Analytics (`G-ZHETPL7EV5`). The build scripts add it. New pages need it too.
- Every page needs Open Graph tags and a 1200x630 preview image (`preview.png` or `cousins-preview.png`) for link previews.
- Light mode only. No dark-mode rules (`prefers-color-scheme`) in any article, so every reader sees the same colors. The dark map stage and dark home page are fixed designs, not a dark mode.
- Phones matter. Check every change at about 390px wide for sideways overflow before pushing.
- Copy style: short plain sentences, no em dashes.

## Cousins map notes

- `data.js` holds the dataset: 36 regions and 9 color families (`GROUPS`), each region with a population series per family in thousands at anchor years, plus chapters, events and migration arcs. `node src/cousins/check.js` prints world totals by year to compare against published figures.
- The 29 communities (`COMMUNITIES`: Polish, Litvak, Moroccan, Iraqi and so on) sit under the families. `SPLIT` says which community each family's people in a region belong to, as one name or as shares over time. The shares inside immigrant countries (USA, Israel, France and others) are rough estimates. `node src/cousins/check2.js` checks every series has a split and prints totals per community.
- Tapping a community in the legend opens its journey: the map shows only that group, the camera follows it, its past routes stay drawn, and the timeline shows only that group. No river or Sankey chart: Yonatan tried one and disliked it.
- Migration arcs are drawn with width by volume. `FLOW_VOL` in `data.js` holds rough figures for the big known waves (thousands, keyed `from>to>startYear`); other arcs are estimated in `app.js` from destination growth.
- No inner scrolling anywhere in the map stage: the side panel, the timeline and the chapter chips must never get their own scrollbar. No "largest right now" list. The chapter buttons under the timeline are always exactly one row (equal columns), never wrapped to two; they are hidden below 900px.
- `map.json` is generated by `gen_map.js` from the `world-atlas` npm package. It rarely needs regenerating.
- Regional figures before 1800 are a reconstruction and have not been reviewed by a specialist. World totals from 1880 match DellaPergola / AJYB.

## Dogfight notes

- The 3D clips use three.js r128 from cdnjs. Jets, paths and cameras are all in `app.js` (`MV` holds each maneuver: path functions, camera, steps, illustrative readouts). Each stage creates its WebGL context only while on screen, so the page stays under the browser's context limit.
- Blue is always you, red is always the bandit. Speed, altitude and G in the readouts are illustrative numbers that follow the text, not a flight model.
- No game names. The page is written for anyone who has tried a flight simulator.

## Flyers notes

- Data comes from ESPN schedules via sportsdataverse (GitHub release `espn_nba_schedules`, files `nba_schedule_<year>.csv`, year = season end). `src/flyers/travel.py <csv>` computes each team's arena-to-arena route, miles, time zone changes and per-game rest; `pool.py` pools four seasons and fits the logistic model behind chapter 3. `data.json` holds the 2024-25 routes, `cost.json` the pooled road-game splits.
- Map: `map.json` is US states (us-atlas 10m, simplified) plus Canada and Mexico outlines (world-atlas 110m). Paris and Mexico City games sit off the map as edge labels. d3 v7 from cdnjs does the projection.
- Hero animation shows each flight for about six days then fades it; the full-season hairball is a static figure in chapter 1. One color for all teams, amber for the picked team. East/West colors are used only in the bar chart.

## Windsurf notes

- Hebrew page, `dir="rtl"`; charts are `direction:ltr` inside. The home row is in English (title, text and `windsurf/card.png`), the share preview `preview.png` is in Hebrew; both are rendered from `og.html` / `og-en.html` in the windsurf working folder with Playwright. Fixed dark design (same sea palette as the home page), not a dark mode. Women are coral (`--w`), men teal (`--m`), everywhere.
- Data: `src/windsurf/data/*.csv` per `SCHEMA.md` (one row per Israeli per event, plus coverage rows: full / partial / none / not_held). `prep.py` merges name spellings (`ALIAS`), adds Hebrew names (`HEB`) and writes `data.json`; it prints any athlete without a Hebrew name. `analyze.py` prints the per-cycle tables.
- Seven chapters, no chapter numbers. Coverage and caveats (entries vs finishers in 2022-2025, Eilat 2016 silver fleet, 2012, Reuveny 41st) live as two bullets in the methodology section; the full results table is collapsed behind a `<details>`. Youth chapter shows counts, plus the youth-to-senior conversion finding (7 of 33 women medalists reached a senior top 10, 4 of 40 men).
- Charts explain themselves with legends (`.key`), not captions under the chart. Yonatan removed every caption; do not add them back. Never use the word שדה for field size, say מתחרים or תחרות.
- Hero chart: every senior result by year and position (log scale). Uniform dots, Europeans hollow, Olympics a diamond, a medal is a thin gold ring around the marker (gender color stays visible, no medal colors, no glow), no best-result line, six one-line annotations (`ANN`, desktop only: the five Olympic medals, Korzits, 2026) and a depth strip (Israelis in the top 10 per year). Hovering a dot highlights that person's career and shows a summary in the tooltip; the caption text never changes.

## Winter notes

- Hebrew page, `dir="rtl"`, light design (paper `#f6f3ec`, one highlight red `#e0482b`). Headline font Rubik 800, body Heebo. Charts are `direction:ltr` inside; Hebrew SVG labels go through `txt()`, which sets rtl and swaps start/end anchors.
- Data: `data/cohort_figures.csv` (study figures, one row per number, source per row), `data/israel_hosp_weekly.csv` (MoH weekly new RSV/flu inpatients, all ages, gaps left empty, 13 hospitals from Oct 2024), `data/cdc_ilinet_age.csv` (CDC ILINet ages 0-4, 2019-20 on). `prep.py` writes `data.json`.
- Hero: a simulated gan of 16 (`simGan`). Model, not data: 7.7 episodes a year, median 9.7 days, season shape from US 0-4. Contagion buttons are an illustration; `KB` factors keep the winter average equal across levels (calibrated over 1,500 winters). Say so on the page, never present it as measured.
- Israeli weekly data by age exists only as chart images in the MoH PDFs, so it is not used. Missing weeks show as a short gray dash, never filled.
- Share images: `node src/winter/og.js` renders `winter/preview.png` (Hebrew) and `winter/card.png` (English home row). Reel: `src/social/reel/winter/reel.js`.

## Adding a new article

1. Make a folder at the root (`/name/`) with `index.html` and a `preview.png`.
2. Add the GA tag, OG tags, and the "← All stories" link.
3. Add a row to `index.html` and a line to `README.md`.
